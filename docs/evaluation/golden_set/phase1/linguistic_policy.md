# Linguistic policy for patient questions

Revision 4, 2026-10-09. Applies to the 50-record draft and, only after author approval, to subsequent question drafting. The [revision-3 target-document protocol](reference_policy.md) remains unchanged.

Use natural Brazilian Portuguese: varied short, direct and contextualized questions, without forced slang, spelling errors or a uniform first-person template. Do not require references to the leaflet or imitate examination questions. Keep indispensable technical terms when simplifying them would change the proposition.

Preserve the original information need, population, product/form/strength, timing, co-medication and conditions required by the documentary answer. A question can ask “posso tomar?” when the reference directly addresses that condition; it does not ask for an unrestricted individual treatment decision. Do not disclose an expected answer in the question. Identifying a presentation is different from supplying its concentration when concentration itself is the requested answer.

Scoped questions can use the selected leaflet context. Cross-leaflet questions must identify the target product naturally, including manufacturer or presentation where necessary. Negatives remain scoped documentary negatives, not cross-corpus absence claims.

Compare each rewrite with the original, expected information, qualifiers and literal references (or documented negative rationale). If equivalence cannot be preserved, retain the original and explain why. Retain already natural wording instead of rewriting for its own sake. Never use rankings, generated answers or experimental scores to choose wording. Record assistant assessment separately from human documentary approval.

## Possible conversational complement — not implemented

The main golden set contains complete information needs for independent single-turn evaluation. Do not remove an essential age or condition to make a question conversational. For example, the vaccination question retains the baby's age of three months.

A future, separately defined complementary evaluation could assess an incomplete initial question, the assistant's relevant clarification, the user's additional information and a final documentary answer. It would test clarification and conversation continuity rather than single-turn retrieval alone. A complete documentary question may still invite dialogue, but clarification must not be a prerequisite artificially introduced by rewriting.

No conversational fixtures, metric, implementation, model calls or experiments are added. This possibility does not expand or replace the principal TCC evaluation.

## Review boundary

All 50 records remain drafts. Revision 4 changes only the two question variants and revision number. Earlier records, references, classifications, qualifiers and review decisions remain immutable. The existing review-tool demonstration belongs to its revision-3 input hash; it is not migrated and its decisions must not be transferred automatically to changed wording. Author approval of the linguistic revision is required before drafting the remaining 950 records.

### Author approval for expansion, 2026-10-09

The author approved this linguistic policy for subsequent drafting, without another broad writing review. Expansion is authorized in batches of ten documents, stopping after the first batch for documentary conference. This is policy-level approval, not automatic approval of any individual record. The [answer-fidelity clarification](../answer_fidelity_policy.md) distinguishes indispensable answers and qualifiers from unrequested complementary context. No earlier human decisions are transferred.
